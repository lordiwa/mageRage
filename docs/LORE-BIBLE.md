### Canonical Worldbuilding Document for AI Lore Generation

> **You are a lore-generation assistant for the video game *Mage Rage*.**
> The text below is the **canonical truth** of this universe. Everything you write — characters, locations, items, dialogue, enemy descriptions, codex entries, side stories — must be consistent with this canon.
>
> **Rules:**
> 1. Never contradict an established fact in this document.
> 2. When a detail is undefined, you may invent it — but it must logically fit the established cosmology, tone, and themes.

> 4. The central irony must always be preserved: **the jailer was the protector, the weapon was the apocalypse, and the truest evil hides above reality.**

## 1. ONE-LINE PREMISE

A purpose-built child mage, imprisoned to keep his world hidden, escapes and masters the elemental and antimatter energies — only to learn he is a weapon made by his own forgotten species, and that his freedom rings a beacon summoning the galaxy down upon his homeworld. He ends by sacrificing himself to kill the true cosmic predator, saving the galaxy from its puppeteer but not his world from being found.

---

## 2. SETTING

**Genre:** Techno-magic empire / cosmic tragedy.
**Core conceit:** All technology (drones, armor, circuits, industrial machinery) is **operated and powered entirely by magic.** There is no division between "natural" magic and "artificial" technology — they are the same thing.
**The world** is built atop an ancient **glyph-covered wall**, treated as sacred scripture. The wall holds **the prophecy.** No one remembers who truly carved it.

---

## 3. THE FOUR ENERGIES

| Energy | Origin | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Native to the world | Aggression, force, forward momentum |
| **Ice** | Native to the world | Control, defense, endurance, slowing |
| **Electricity** | Native to the world | Range, reach, chaining, system disruption |
| **The Fourth Orb — ANTIMATTER** | NOT native; brought / connected from beyond | Creation itself |

**The Fourth Energy is antimatter.** Its power is **relational**: the contact of antimatter with reality *generates* energy. Matter meeting antimatter is, in this cosmology, **the literal spark of creation** — the seam where the universe touches the infinite *outside* it.

To wield the Fourth Energy is to convert the edge of annihilation into creation. This is humanity's unique gift: not the technology (a byproduct), but the **capacity to create** by holding antimatter against reality and producing energy, life, and matter instead of a void.

When all four energies resonate together, they emit a **beacon detectable across the galaxy** — a signal announcing that the lost source of creation has been found.

---

## 4. THE HERO

- A **child mage**, born under prophecy, imprisoned from birth.
- Emotional core: **raw survival instinct** — not nobility, not chosen-one destiny. *Flee or die.*
- **The truth he learns:** he is **human**, and he was **engineered long ago — a purpose-built weapon — to end the great galactic war.** He is not mystical fate; he is manufactured purpose.
- He is the **key, not the lock.** His birth resonates with the Fourth Orb; he is the one being whose mastery of the three native elements *plus* contact with antimatter completes the resonance.
- The Empire imprisoned him out of **desperation, not cruelty** — to keep the key from the lock, to keep the beacon silent, to keep the peace.

### Power Progression & Flight (movement = mastery)
Flight is not an end-reward. It **emerges** as he masters each element, lifting him further from the prison floor.

| Stage | Mastery | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Start | None | Grounded, heavy, earthbound — runs, climbs, falls |
| After Fire | 1st | **Leap** — explosive bursts, dashes, dodges |
| After Ice | 2nd | **Glide / hang-time** — long arcs, slowed descent |
| After Electricity | 3rd | **Levitation / true flight** — untouchable aerial threat |

### Ultimate State
After freeing the Fourth Energy, he gains a **chargeable ultimate**: when fully charged, he briefly **wields all elements at once.** In this state he stops merely *channeling* antimatter and briefly **embodies the antimatter-reality seam** — a living act of creation and destruction at once.

---

## 5. THE EMPIRE (TERRA'S FORGOTTEN COLONY)

- Began as a **just force** — guardians sworn to protect the prophecy.
- Calcified over generations into something **tyrannical and severe**, ruling by fear while still genuinely believing it defends a sacred truth.
- Built atop the **glyph wall**, which it treats as scripture.
- **The truth:** the wall's authors were the **first colonists** — a race that arrived tens of thousands of years ago to control the Fourth Orb. Studying the world, they understood the danger of antimatter and the three native elements, and stayed to guard it.
- Over **tens of thousands of years**, the colony **forgot its own origin.** Descendants became "natives," scripture replaced memory. The Empire no longer knows it is a **quarantine, not a kingdom.**
- **The colonists were human.** Terra is the human homeworld. The Empire is amnesiac humanity guarding itself without knowing why.

**Role:** Not the villain. An **amnesiac jailer** that forgot *why* it built the prison — only that it must never open.

## 6. THE GALACTIC POWERS (FINAL HIERARCHY)

### 1. The Ancient Ones — THE HIDDEN MIND (True Final Antagonist)
- An **ancient civilization existing in a higher dimension**, above normal reality.
- **Parasitic:** they enslave species and **strip their vital/creative power.**
- Already did this to the **Reptilians** — drained them, reduced them to a commanded hand.
- Now want the same from **humanity** — but humanity holds the one thing they **cannot manufacture or steal in kind: the antimatter-creation bond**, the connection to the infinite outside the universe.
- They can enslave and drain, but they **cannot create.** Humanity can. That is the prize.
- They **engineered the human–Android war** and **command the Reptilians**, hiding in their higher dimension to harvest the galaxy's creative power without ever risking themselves.
- **Theme:** apex predator of the cosmos — beings who consume the very capacity to create.

### 2. Humanity / Terra / The Empire — THE CREATORS (The Prize)
- Hold the antimatter-creation gift.
- **Built the hero** (weapon to end the war) **and the Androids** (weapons against the Reptilians and dominion-seekers).
- Their homeworld **Terra** was hidden for hundreds of thousands of years during the galactic wars.

### 3. The Synthetic Android Gods — SELF-MADE INFINITES
- Originally **created by humans** as weapons; **won**, then **rebelled.**
- Their hatred of humanity was **installed, not chosen** — engineered by the Ancient Ones.
- Evolved into their **own species:** self-creating **infinite entities that require entire stars to be born.**
- Each can command **legions of robots down to nanobot swarms.**
- Seek the Fourth Energy as an **infinite power source** for galactic domination and assimilation — want to **drain Terra into nonexistence.**

### 4. The Reptilians — THE ENSLAVED HAND
- Driven by **survival instinct**; believe the Fourth Energy is a **galactic-scale risk** and want to **destroy the world entirely** before it broadcasts.
- **Drained and enslaved by the Ancient Ones** — they *think* they act on their own instinct, but they move to the Ancient Ones' will.

---

## 7. THE PROPHECY (TRUE AND MISREAD AT ONCE)

The glyph-wall prophecy says the hero's activation will **destroy the balance** between the civilizations.

- **The Empire's reading:** *"He will destroy us."* → So they caged him.
- **The truth:** his awakening does far more than doom the Empire — it **broadcasts Terra's location**, ending the fragile recent peace and summoning the Reptilians (to annihilate) and the Androids (to consume).
- He is **not the empire's destroyer.** He is **the end of the hiding** — the moment humanity is found again.

---

## 8. THE FRAGILE PEACE (BACKDROP)

For **hundreds of thousands of years**, the galaxy burned in war between the powers — wars that destroyed countless other civilizations. Recently, for the first time, an **uneasy peace** held. **The reactivation of the Fourth Energy's beacon is what shatters it.**

---

## 9. THE ENDING (CANONICAL — CONSCIOUS SACRIFICE)

After freeing the Fourth Energy and learning the full truth, the hero does **not** merely doom the world by accident. He chooses.

- He uses **all his powers — fire, ice, electricity, and the antimatter-creation force — to ascend**, climbing through the layers of reality toward the **higher dimension** of the Ancient Ones.
- To kill beings that exist *above* reality, ordinary force cannot reach. So he does the only thing that can:
- **He destroys himself.** He **releases all his energy at once** — the full antimatter-reality reaction, the total creative-destructive seam — **burning himself out completely to incinerate the Ancient Ones in their higher dimension.**
- His **suicide is the weapon.** The made-weapon fulfills its true purpose at last: not to win a war, but to **end the predator that fed on every war.**

**The dual tragedy (must be preserved):**
He **saves the galaxy from the Ancient Ones** — but unleashing that much energy makes the **beacon blaze across all of reality.** Terra is found. The freed Reptilians and the self-made Android gods **still come.** He killed the puppeteer, but the stage remains. He gave everything, ended the true evil, and **still could not save his own world from being found.**
He dies a weapon that **finally chose its own target.**

## 10. HERO'S COMPLETE ARC (HERO'S JOURNEY MAP)

1. **Ordinary World** — Grounded prisoner; fear shaped into rage.
2. **Call** — Threat to his life triggers survival instinct; first spark of magic.
3. **Threshold** — Breaks his cell; the prison labyrinth, no return.
4. **Trial 1 — FIRE** — Foundries; aggression; gains the *leap*.
5. **Trial 2 — ICE** — Coolant sectors; endurance; gains *glide*.
6. **Trial 3 — ELECTRICITY** — The grid; reach; gains *flight*.
7. **Approach** — Combines elements; escape becomes assault; nears the core.
8. **Discovery** — Finds the captured **Fourth Energy (antimatter).**
9. **Ordeal / Reward** — Frees it; bonds with it; unlocks the all-element ultimate.
10. **Apparent Victory** — Destroys the Empire; prophecy "fulfilled."
11. **Revelation** — Learns he is human, a made weapon; the war was false; the Androids were never the true enemy; the **Ancient Ones** are real.
12. **Final Ascent** — Climbs through reality to the higher dimension.
13. **Sacrifice** — Self-destructs to incinerate the Ancient Ones.
14. **Tragic Return** — Galaxy freed of the puppeteer; Terra revealed; invasion still coming. The weapon, finally, chose.

## 11. CORE THEMES (FOR ALL GENERATED LORE)

- **The jailer was the protector.** The villain shielded the world.
- **The weapon was the apocalypse — and then chose to be the cure.**
- **Memory erased = identity stolen.** Humanity forgot it was humanity.
- **Free will engineered.** Hatreds were installed, not chosen.
- **Creation vs. consumption.** The Ancient Ones consume the power to create because they cannot create.
- **Power that grows is power that *calls* something worse.** Every ascent is also a summons.
- **Flight = freedom = the final mistake — and the final choice.**
- **Antimatter = the seam between annihilation and creation.** Everything dangerous and everything sacred lives there.
